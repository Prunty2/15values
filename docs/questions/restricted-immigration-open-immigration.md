# Restricted Immigration vs Open Immigration

Status: Locked in.

Statements are listed in agreed inclusion-priority order.

1. Permanent immigration should be subject to a strict annual numerical limit, based on housing availability and workforce deficits.
2. People should generally be allowed to settle in another country to improve their living conditions.
3. Only applicants with skills considered important to my country should normally qualify for permanent residence.
4. Partners and dependent children of immigrants who are permanently settled in my country should generally be allowed to join them.
5. Refugees fleeing their home countries should be allowed to settle permanently, regardless of their skills or cultural compatibility.
6. People who migrate illegally should be allowed to remain in my country rather than face deportation.
7. Permanent immigration should be limited even when employers want to recruit more workers from abroad.
8. Immigration should be reduced to ease pressure on housing and public services, even when additional arrivals would bring economic benefits.
9. Family immigration should count towards the same annual numerical limit as other permanent immigration.
10. People with a confirmed job offer should generally be allowed to move to my country for work.
11. Applicants for permanent settlement should need sponsorship from an employer or family member.
12. Graduating from one of my country’s universities should not, by itself, provide a route to permanent residence.
13. People fleeing persecution should be allowed to enter and receive protection even when ordinary immigration limits have been reached.
14. The government should prioritise migrants from culturally similar countries.
15. My country should allow non-citizen migrants to receive welfare benefits or live in social housing.
16. My country should allow more migrants from diverse backgrounds because their cultures differ from our own.

## Agreement direction

- Restricted: 1, 3, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14.
- Open: 2, 4, 5, 6, 10, 13, 15, 16.
