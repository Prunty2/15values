# Nationalism vs Internationalism

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

1. My country’s institutions should have final authority over laws that apply within its borders.
2. International institutions should have authority to make binding decisions for my country, even when my government opposes those decisions.
3. Courts in my country should have final authority when national law conflicts with an international court’s ruling.
4. My country should make long-term commitments to international alliances, even when those commitments limit its freedom to act independently.
5. My country should be able to withdraw from international commitments without needing approval from international institutions.
6. International courts should have binding authority over disputes involving my government, including cases brought against its own officials.
7. My country should prioritise its own interests when they conflict with goals set by international institutions.
8. Countries should give international institutions decision-making authority over environmental problems that cross national borders.
9. My country should remain outside international blocs that require members to adopt a common foreign policy.
10. International human-rights institutions should be able to require changes to my country’s laws.
11. My country should keep control of its currency rather than transfer monetary policy to a shared international authority.
12. My country should pool funding and decision-making with other countries to manage shared international problems.
13. My country should retain sole authority over entry at its borders rather than share that authority with international institutions.
14. My country should accept common trade rules negotiated with other countries rather than set all its trade rules independently.
15. My country should keep control of national policy rather than join an international political federation.
16. My country should join international efforts to enforce collectively agreed rules, even when staying neutral would better serve its immediate interests.

## Agreement direction

- Nationalism: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Internationalism: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
