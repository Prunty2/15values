# Planning vs Free Market

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

These statements concern economic direction and market decisions, not business ownership.

1. Businesses in my country should generally decide what to produce without government direction.
2. My government should set compulsory production targets for economically important industries.
3. Prices in my country should generally be determined by supply and demand rather than set by government.
4. My government should direct investment towards sectors chosen in a national economic plan.
5. Businesses in my country should expand or close according to consumer demand rather than government production plans.
6. My government should control the prices of essential goods, even when suppliers would prefer to charge more.
7. Businesses in my country should be able to enter an industry without having to prove to the government that more production is needed.
8. My government should direct banks to lend to industries it considers national priorities.
9. People and businesses should be able to move their money abroad without government approval.
10. My government should subsidise selected industries to steer economic development, even when private investors would favour other sectors.
11. Privately issued currencies should be allowed to compete with government-issued money.
12. My government should set a legal minimum wage rather than leave the lowest wages entirely to negotiation between employers and workers.
13. Wages in my country should generally be set through negotiations between employers and workers rather than government wage schedules.
14. My government should direct central-bank lending and interest-rate policy to support its national economic plan.
15. Rental prices in my country should be set by market demand rather than government rent controls.
16. My government should coordinate national production and distribution during ordinary economic conditions rather than leave them to individual businesses.

## Agreement direction

- Planning: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
- Free Market: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
