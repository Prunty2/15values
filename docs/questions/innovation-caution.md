# Innovation vs Caution

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

1. My country should allow new technologies to be widely used when early evidence suggests substantial benefits, even if long-term risks remain uncertain.
2. New technologies should remain limited to small trials until there is substantial evidence about their long-term risks.
3. My country should approve major infrastructure projects when their public benefits are substantial, even if some natural habitats are permanently lost.
4. New medical technologies should not become widely available until their long-term safety has been studied.
5. My country should adopt AI systems that raise workplace productivity, even if many existing jobs are replaced and workers must retrain.
6. Preserving undisturbed habitats should take priority over development projects, even when the projects would bring substantial public benefits.
7. My country should support replacing some conventional meat production with cultivated meat, even before its long-term environmental effects are fully understood.
8. Brain implants intended to enhance the abilities of healthy people should remain limited to research until there is strong evidence of long-term safety.
9. My country should prioritise technological solutions to climate change over reducing energy and material consumption, even when those technologies are not yet proven at large scale.
10. Major construction projects should be delayed while their environmental effects remain poorly understood, even when the projects are expected to benefit the public.
11. Adults should be allowed to use brain implants to enhance their mental abilities once basic safety requirements are met, even if long-term effects remain uncertain.
12. AI systems used to make medical diagnoses should remain limited to supervised trials until there is strong evidence of their reliability across different patient groups.
13. My country should allow controlled trials of embryo gene editing to enhance human abilities, rather than limit gene editing to preventing disease.
14. Genetically modified crops should require evidence of long-term ecosystem safety before widespread cultivation, even if early trials show higher yields.
15. My country should prioritise expanding housing and transport infrastructure over preserving undeveloped land when the two conflict.
16. My country should delay technologies that could cause irreversible harm until there is strong evidence that the harm is unlikely, even when early evidence suggests substantial benefits.

## Agreement direction

- Innovation: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Caution: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
