# Protectionism vs Free Trade

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

1. My country should use import tariffs to protect domestic businesses from foreign competition.
2. My country should remove most barriers to international trade, even when some domestic businesses lose sales.
3. My government should favour domestic suppliers when purchasing goods, even when foreign suppliers offer lower prices.
4. Businesses in my country should be free to buy goods and materials from whichever country offers the best terms.
5. My country should restrict imports of essential goods to preserve domestic production, even when imported goods cost less.
6. My country should reduce its own trade barriers even when other countries do not do the same.
7. My country should temporarily restrict imports to help new domestic industries become established, even when this raises prices for consumers.
8. Foreign investors should be allowed to establish businesses on the same terms as domestic investors, even when local firms face more competition.
9. My country should restrict agricultural imports to preserve domestic farming capacity.
10. Domestic businesses should adjust to foreign competition rather than receive government trade protection.
11. My country should impose tariffs in response to tariffs placed on its exports.
12. Foreign and domestic products should face the same product taxes in my country.
13. My country should restrict imports from foreign firms that receive large subsidies from their governments.
14. Trade agreements signed by my country should remove tariffs, even when this increases dependence on foreign suppliers.
15. My country should prevent foreign investors from gaining control of strategic businesses, even when they offer more investment than domestic buyers.
16. People in my country should be free to buy online services from foreign providers without special trade restrictions.

## Agreement direction

- Protectionism: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Free Trade: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
