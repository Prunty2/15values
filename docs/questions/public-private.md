# Public vs Private

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

These statements concern public versus private ownership and provision. Public funding does not necessarily imply public ownership; contracting a service to a private provider does not necessarily transfer ownership of public assets.

1. Most businesses in my country should be privately owned.
2. Essential utility networks in my country, such as water supply networks, should be publicly owned.
3. Private ownership should be preferred when public and private providers could offer comparable cost, quality, and access.
4. My country should establish publicly owned businesses in competitive industries rather than leave those industries entirely to private owners.
5. Healthcare facilities in my country should generally be privately owned, even when treatment is publicly funded.
6. Passenger rail infrastructure in my country should be publicly owned.
7. Publicly funded services should be contracted to privately owned providers when they can offer comparable quality and access at lower cost.
8. My country should have publicly owned banks alongside private banks.
9. My country should sell publicly owned commercial businesses to private owners.
10. Public bodies should own a substantial share of rental housing in my country.
11. Businesses important to national security should remain privately owned when government regulation can protect security interests.
12. Businesses extracting natural resources in my country should generally be publicly owned.
13. Public bodies should contract routine services, such as waste collection, to privately owned businesses rather than operate their own providers.
14. The government should retain ownership of strategic businesses even when selling them would raise substantial public revenue.
15. A publicly owned business that repeatedly needs financial support should be sold to private owners rather than kept in public ownership.
16. My country should own a national broadcaster rather than leave broadcasting entirely to private owners.

## Agreement direction

- Public: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
- Private: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
