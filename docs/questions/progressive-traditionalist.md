# Progressive vs Traditionalist

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

1. Social customs in my country should be open to substantial change as people’s values change.
2. Preserving long-standing social traditions should take priority over adapting them to new values.
3. Families outside the traditional two-parent married household should receive the same social recognition as that household.
4. Society should continue to encourage distinct traditional roles for men and women.
5. Marriage in my country should be available to couples regardless of their sex.
6. Society should continue to favour the two-parent married household as its preferred family model.
7. Choosing an abortion early in pregnancy should be socially accepted.
8. Parents should have the final say over whether their children receive school lessons that conflict with the family’s traditional sexual values.
9. Same-sex couples should be as socially accepted as adoptive parents as opposite-sex couples.
10. Traditional expectations about modest dress should continue to guide how people dress in public.
11. Language conventions should change to reflect how people identify themselves, even when this departs from traditional usage.
12. Passing established moral customs to the next generation should take priority over encouraging new ones.
13. Adults who take on roles traditionally associated with the other sex should receive the same social acceptance as those who follow traditional roles.
14. Changes to long-standing social institutions should be gradual, even when faster change is popular.
15. Schools should teach pupils about different sexual orientations and gender identities, even when these differ from traditional family expectations.
16. Long-standing customs should be preserved for their connection to earlier generations, even when their practical purpose is no longer clear.

## Agreement direction

- Progressive: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Traditionalist: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
