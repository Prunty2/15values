# Assimilation vs Multiculturalism

Statements are listed in agreed inclusion-priority order.

1. The government should prioritise the national culture over migrant cultures when they conflict.
2. Public services should accommodate different cultural practices, even when doing so requires additional public spending.
3. People who settle permanently in a country should be expected to adopt its cultural customs.
4. Becoming part of the national community should not require giving up a foreign cultural identity.
5. Public institutions should prioritise using the national language over providing services in several foreign languages.
6. National ceremonies should acknowledge Indigenous peoples within the nation, even when they make up a very small share of the population.
7. Government cultural funding should primarily support my country’s predominant traditions.
8. Distinct cultural identities should take priority over a shared national culture when their expectations conflict.
9. Public institutions should organise their schedules around one common cultural calendar rather than accommodate different communities’ celebrations.
10. The government should recognise several cultural traditions as part of my country’s national identity.
11. Integration programmes should encourage migrants to participate in the wider national community rather than live mainly within culturally separate communities.
12. Public signage should be written in foreign languages to accommodate migrants within their communities.
13. Families should give greater emphasis to a shared national identity than to a distinct community identity.
14. Full body coverings, such as the burqa, should remain legal even when other full body coverings are prohibited.
15. My country’s national identity should include several distinct cultural ways of life.
16. Government integration programmes should emphasise adopting shared national customs rather than maintaining distinct community customs.

## Agreement direction

- Assimilation: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 16.
- Multiculturalism: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 15.
