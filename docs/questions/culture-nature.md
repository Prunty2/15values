# Culture vs Nature

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

These statements concern beliefs about what primarily explains human traits and behaviour, not preferred policies or scientific knowledge. Social influences include upbringing, learning, and cultural surroundings. Biological influences include inherited characteristics and bodily development. The statements compare relative influence rather than ask whether either influence exists.

1. People’s behaviour is shaped more by upbringing and social surroundings than by inherited tendencies.
2. Differences in IQ scores between countries can be explained more by inherited characteristics than by upbringing and education.
3. Where men and women behave differently, social expectations and upbringing explain more of the difference than biology does.
4. Differences in people’s sensitivity to stress are explained more by biological differences than by social surroundings and experience.
5. Differences in people’s interests are shaped more by experiences and opportunities than by inherited tendencies.
6. People’s willingness to take risks is shaped more by inherited tendencies than by learning and experience.
7. Whether people develop cooperative habits depends more on their social surroundings than on inherited tendencies.
8. Differences in people’s capacity for empathy are explained more by inherited characteristics than by social experience and upbringing.
9. Differences in people’s ability to sustain attention are explained more by their experiences and surroundings than by inherited characteristics.
10. People’s immediate reactions to what they consider right or wrong are shaped more by biological predispositions than by upbringing and culture.
11. Differences in what people find threatening are explained more by learning and experience than by inherited tendencies.
12. Differences in people’s temperament are explained more by biological predispositions than by upbringing and social surroundings.
13. People’s preferences about physical attractiveness are shaped more by culture and personal experience than by inherited tendencies.
14. Different races being more likely to commit violent acts (per capita) is better explained by biological differences than by upbringing and social surroundings.
15. People’s habits of self-control are shaped more by learning and experience than by inherited racial tendencies.
16. The gender pay gap can be attributed to differences in economic value between men and women instead of different social expectations.

## Agreement direction

- Culture: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Nature: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
