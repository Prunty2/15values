# Authority vs Liberty

Status: Locked in.

Statements are listed in agreed inclusion-priority order.

1. Adults should be allowed to make personal choices such as drug use that risk personal wellbeing when they do not directly harm others.
2. Authorities should be allowed to monitor private communications when a court authorises the surveillance to investigate serious crime.
3. Peaceful public demonstrations should be allowed even when they temporarily obstruct roads or access to businesses, provided emergency access remains available.
4. People should be allowed to criticise public institutions, even when their criticism causes widespread offence.
5. The government should be able to require adults to follow health measures intended primarily to protect those adults themselves.
6. Police should be allowed to keep records of people’s movements for several years, even when those people are not suspected of an offence.
7. Police should be allowed to stop drivers at random and test them for intoxication, even without a reason to suspect impairment.
8. Authorities should be able to restrict a person’s movements before they commit an offence when a court finds a serious risk that they will harm others.
9. Adults should be allowed to access books, films, and websites that the government considers morally objectionable.
10. People should be allowed to use online communication services without having to verify their identity with the government.
11. Police should be able to stop and search people without individual suspicion when there is a credible threat of serious violence in the area.
12. Authorities should be able to prohibit a public gathering when there is credible evidence that it is likely to lead to serious disorder.
13. Police should need a court warrant to search someone’s home without their consent, except when there is an immediate threat to someone’s safety.
14. People should be allowed to publish confidential government information that reveals official misconduct.
15. Adults should be allowed to own firearms for personal defence, subject to checks for a history of violence.
16. Authorities should be allowed to hold people suspected of terrorism without independent court review until they consider the threat resolved.

## Agreement direction

- Authority: 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 16.
- Liberty: 1, 3, 4, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15.
