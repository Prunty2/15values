# Authority vs Liberty

Status: Locked in.

Statements are listed in inclusion-priority order. Revised with owner authorisation on 6 October 2026; bank 4.0.0 preserves all agreement directions.

1. People should be allowed to criticise public institutions, even when their criticism causes widespread offence.
2. Authorities should be allowed to collect people’s private communication records in bulk to help prevent serious crime, even when those people are not individually suspected of an offence.
3. Adults should be allowed to own firearms for personal defence, subject to checks for a history of violence.
4. Adults should be allowed to make personal choices such as drug use that risk personal wellbeing when they do not directly harm others.
5. The government should be able to require adults to follow health measures intended primarily to protect those adults themselves.
6. Police should be allowed to keep records of people’s movements for several years, even when those people are not suspected of an offence.
7. The government should be able to make vaccination against a serious infectious disease a condition for adults to enter shared public spaces during an outbreak.
8. Authorities should be able to place people assessed by a court as posing a serious risk of violence under preventive movement restrictions, with regular judicial review.
9. Adults should be allowed to access books, films, and websites that the government considers morally objectionable.
10. People should be allowed to use online communication services without having to verify their identity with the government.
11. Police should be allowed to stop and search people without individual suspicion in a designated area for a limited period when there is credible evidence of a serious threat to public safety.
12. The government should be able to prohibit public expression that incites hatred against people because of their race or religion, even when it does not directly threaten violence.
13. Peaceful public demonstrations should be allowed even when they temporarily obstruct roads or access to businesses, provided emergency access remains available.
14. People should be allowed to publish confidential government information that reveals official misconduct.
15. Police should need a court warrant to search someone’s home without their consent, except when there is an immediate threat to someone’s safety.
16. Authorities should be allowed to hold people suspected of terrorism without independent court review until they consider the threat resolved.

## Agreement direction

- Authority: 2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 11, 12, 16.
- Liberty: 1, 3, 4, 9, 10, 13, 14, 15.
