# High Redistribution vs Low Redistribution

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

1. My country should use taxes and benefits to substantially reduce differences in disposable income.
2. Reducing income differences should not, by itself, justify taking more income through taxation.
3. My country should provide a stronger minimum income through benefits, even when this requires higher taxes.
4. Income benefits should provide a basic safety net rather than substantially narrow differences in living standards.
5. Higher earners in my country should pay a substantially larger share of their income in tax than lower earners.
6. People in my country should retain more of their earnings, even if this leaves larger income differences between households.
7. My country should tax large inheritances to fund income support for people with fewer resources.
8. Income support for working-age adults who can work should have a time limit, even if some recipients still have low incomes when it ends.
9. My country should tax accumulated wealth to fund transfers to households with low incomes.
10. Income benefits should be kept modest so that those funding them face a lower tax burden.
11. People eligible for income benefits should receive enough support to participate in ordinary social life, beyond meeting basic survival needs.
12. My country should prioritise cutting taxes over increasing cash benefits.
13. My country should provide publicly funded income supplements to low-paid workers.
14. Additional income earned by high earners should not face higher tax rates than additional income earned by other workers.
15. My country should redistribute some large landholdings to people with little or no land, compensating owners through public funds.
16. Cash benefits should be restricted to households unable to meet basic needs rather than extended to a wider range of low-income households.

## Agreement direction

- High Redistribution: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Low Redistribution: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.

## Format balance

| Format | High Redistribution | Low Redistribution |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Short: first 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Medium: first 5 | 3 | 2 |
| Long: first 9 | 5 | 4 |
| Comprehensive: all 16 | 8 | 8 |
