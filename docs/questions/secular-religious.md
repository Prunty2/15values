# Secular vs Religious

Status: Draft.

Statements are listed in proposed inclusion-priority order.

This axis concerns religion’s role in government, law, and public institutions, not personal religious belief.

1. My country’s laws should be justified without relying on the teachings of any particular religion.
2. Religious teachings should be an official source of national law in my country.
3. My government should make policy without needing approval from religious leaders.
4. My country’s constitution should recognise a particular religion as a foundation of the state.
5. Public schools in my country should teach about religions without trying to persuade pupils to adopt one.
6. Public schools in my country should include instruction intended to cultivate faith in the state’s recognised religion.
7. My country’s state courts should apply laws enacted by civil institutions rather than rulings issued by religious authorities.
8. Religious authorities should have an official role in reviewing proposed laws before my government enacts them.
9. Official ceremonies held by my government should not include organised religious worship.
10. My government should use public funds for activities intended to strengthen religious belief.
11. Government buildings should not display symbols endorsing a particular religion.
12. My country should keep national public holidays marking the predominant religion’s major festivals as an official recognition of that religion.
13. Religious leaders should not hold government seats solely because of their religious office.
14. Religious organisations should receive tax exemptions specifically for their religious activities, beyond exemptions available for charitable work.
15. My country should remove religious approval requirements from laws governing marriage.
16. My country should recognise decisions made by religious courts as legally binding in some areas of family law.

## Agreement direction

- Secular: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15.
- Religious: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16.
