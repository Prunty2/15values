# Democracy vs Autocracy

Status: Locked in.

Statements are listed in agreed inclusion-priority order.

1. National leaders should need to win regular competitive elections to remain in office.
2. National governments should be chosen by a panel of experts rather than by the general electorate.
3. Every adult citizen should have the right to vote, regardless of their education or intelligence.
4. The head of government should be able to enact national laws even when parliament votes against them.
5. Parties opposing the government should be allowed to campaign and stand in national elections under the same conditions as the incumbent government.
6. Opposition representatives should be able to investigate the government’s use of public money.
7. Courts independent of the government should be able to overturn government decisions that violate the constitution.
8. National governments should be chosen by trade union leaders rather than by the general electorate.
9. The governing leadership should have authority to exclude parties from national elections because of their political positions.
10. Voters should be able to repeal a law through a binding referendum.
11. National leaders should have sole authority over major national policy decisions.
12. Elected representatives should be able to remove the head of government for serious abuse of power through a defined constitutional process.
13. A national leader should be able to postpone scheduled elections without independent approval when they consider political continuity necessary.
14. Voters should be able to remove an elected representative before their term ends through a defined recall vote.
15. National leaders should be able to govern without being accountable to voters or elected representatives.
16. The armed forces should be able to replace a civilian government on their own judgement that it is unfit to govern.

## Agreement direction

- Democracy: 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 14.
- Autocracy: 2, 4, 8, 9, 11, 13, 15, 16.
