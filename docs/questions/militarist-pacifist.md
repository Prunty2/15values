# Militarist vs Pacifist

Status: Locked in.

Statements are listed in agreed inclusion-priority order.

1. My country should be willing to use military force to secure national interests, even when it has not been attacked.
2. Diplomatic measures should take priority over military action, even when they are slower to achieve my country’s aims.
3. My country should be willing to bear substantial financial costs to maintain military superiority over potential opponents.
4. Military action should be permitted to prevent an imminent attack when there is strong evidence that the attack is being prepared.
5. My country should use armed force only to respond to an armed attack against itself or an ally.
6. My country should require male citizens to complete a period of military service to train a large, capable force and deter potential aggressors.
7. My country should accept substantial diplomatic concessions to prevent a war, even when doing so weakens its international position.
8. My country should develop AI-powered weapons that can select and attack targets without a human approving each attack if this would make military operations more efficient and improve national security.
9. Defence policy should focus on repelling attacks rather than conducting military campaigns abroad.
10. My country should avoid war and military action at all costs, even if an ally is being attacked.
11. My country should maintain forces capable of prolonged combat beyond its borders.
12. My country should leave a territorial dispute unresolved rather than start a war to settle it.
13. My country should be willing to threaten military action to obtain concessions in serious international disputes.
14. When the two conflict, reducing the risk of war should take priority over demonstrating military resolve.
15. My country should use military espionage to gain an advantage over potential opponents, even when doing so risks worsening relations with them.
16. My country should accept reductions in its military capabilities as part of negotiated efforts to prevent war.

## Agreement direction

- Militarist: 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 13, 15.
- Pacifist: 2, 5, 7, 9, 10, 12, 14, 16.
