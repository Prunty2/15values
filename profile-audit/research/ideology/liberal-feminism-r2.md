## Contemporary context and counter-evidence

NOW is an advocacy organisation, not the incumbent US government. Current opposition to administration policy is its own expressed position, not proof the government implements feminist doctrine. The page labelled 2026 resolutions contains 2025 deadlines, so an exact adoption date is not invented. Historical resolutions are explicitly dated, and later contrary positions would take precedence. Public healthcare funding does not imply public ownership of facilities; fair trade does not prescribe every tariff mechanism; secular civil law does not prescribe every holiday or symbol; anti-discrimination does not resolve national IQ, stress, attention or inherited temperament.

The latest source reading recovered explicit opposition to mandatory draft registration and welfare time limits, qualified fair-trade and peacekeeping policies, and civil religious separation. Three new zero answers are proposed as documented institutional mixtures, not missing evidence; independent review must verify the exact tradeoff.
