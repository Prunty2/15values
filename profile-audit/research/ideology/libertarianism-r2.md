# Libertarianism (US Libertarian Party tradition): revision 2 research dossier

Research resumed 2026-10-04. Incomplete research draft; no archive or percentage publication authorised by this dossier.

Scope: American individual-rights and free-market libertarianism as articulated in the Libertarian Party platform accessed on the research date, separate from left-libertarian theories and from a strict single-author minarchism. No incumbent government is claimed to implement this doctrine completely. Strict revision-2 re-audit: assess only source-supported positions on the exact questions. Unsupported empirical comparisons, modern technology analogies and institutional particulars remain unknown. Absence of doctrine is not a neutral position. This draft must not be used to calculate or publish a complete profile.

Current coverage: 155 numerical answers and 85 unresolved answers. All 240 exact question records remain in the draft.

## Evidence checked and limitations

The Libertarian Party platform was re-read in full, including sections 1.0–1.9, 2.0–2.14 and 3.1–3.7. Its express rights rules can support policy inferences. Section 1.0 separates freedom from moral approval; section 3.6 expressly endorses initiatives, referenda and recall. Sections 1.1 and 2.13 protect voluntary personal health risks and access to medical procedures.

Searches for platform positions on genetics, intelligence and human nature did not establish comparative behavioural-science answers. General freedom does not establish a position on every social convention. Continued unknowns are evidential gaps, not political neutrality.
