## Scope and evidence boundary

The national-conservative school articulated in the Edmund Burke Foundation statement of 2022; not ethnic supremacism, all nationalisms, or every policy of its signatories. Revision2 is an incomplete evidence audit of this specified school. Unsupported modern applications and empirical causal comparisons are null/unknown. Neither agreement nor neutrality is inferred from silence. Historical or programme sources are not a complete contemporary incumbent-government record.

Hazony’s 2022 interview is interpreted alongside the collective statement, not as every signatory’s personal programme. It supports explicit biblical public morality and intergenerational restoration. Biological inheritance must not be confused with inherited social membership: Hazony’s reference to being born into families and nations is not a genetic account of intelligence, attention or violent behaviour. Private minority protections remain counter-evidence to unrestricted religious coercion.

The draft contains all 240 current question IDs: 86 supported numeric assessments and 154 unresolved entries. Unknown is not a score. No target placement or neighbouring profile was consulted.
