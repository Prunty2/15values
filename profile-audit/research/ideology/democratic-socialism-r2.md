The scope remains the DSA programme, not a composite of democratic-socialist governments worldwide.

Democratic socialism articulated by DSA, including its current democratic socialist republic, social-ownership and abolitionist programme. This is a specific organisation’s doctrinal variant, not all democratic socialism, and not a profile of any incumbent national government. This is a sourced interpretation of the specified tradition, not an adherent questionnaire. Unresolved evidence or an unrepresented institutional premise is recorded as unknown, never as numerical neutrality. Historical sources are not treated as answers to modern technologies by analogy. Government acts are contextual evidence only, not a substitute for the scoped doctrine.

## Counter-evidence and remaining research limits

Nationalising the largest companies does not necessarily mean most companies by number. Equal status is not a claim about the relative biology of IQ, stress or attention. The curriculum calls some human capacities inherent, preventing a blank-slate assumption. DSA’s current national platform does not specify the question bank’s full set of trade, technology and policing thresholds.

Research looked for doctrinal positions rather than contemporary scientific answers to the Culture vs Nature questions. A source’s moral equality claim is not treated as empirical evidence. No score, similarity target or expected ideological location was used. Neutral answers below are explicit mixed or conditional judgements, never placeholders for unknown evidence.

## Changes supported by the new readings

- `culture-nature-07`: None → 1. The official education curriculum explains failures of collective action through social risks and constraints, and presents solidarity as a capacity developed through organising. This supports a qualified social-development account of cooperative habits, rather than inferring it merely from an egalitarian goal.
