# Nazism (German National Socialism, 1920–1945): revision 2 research dossier

Research resumed 2026-10-04. Incomplete research draft; no archive or percentage publication authorised by this dossier.

Scope: The NSDAP programme, Hitler’s 1936 economic memorandum and documented regime practice, interpreted through Holocaust scholarship. Programme rhetoric is distinguished from later private ownership under compulsory state direction. Membership and welfare were racially exclusionary; apparent social provisions are not presented as universal rights. This is a historical doctrine with no claimed incumbent exemplar.  Strict revision-2 re-audit: assess only source-supported positions on the exact questions. Unsupported empirical comparisons, modern technology analogies and institutional particulars remain unknown. Absence of doctrine is not a neutral position. This draft must not be used to calculate or publish a complete profile.

Current coverage: 64 numerical answers and 176 unresolved answers. All 240 exact question records remain in the draft.

## Evidence checked and limitations

USHMM material on teachers and academics was additionally investigated. It documents racial curricula, institutional mobilisation and academic participation in eugenics. These are historical claims about the regime, not endorsements of racial theories.

A regime’s eugenic doctrine does not automatically answer every modern variance comparison. Modern AI, embryo editing, cultivated meat and brain-implant thresholds have no direct historical counterparts in the checked sources. No present government is described as the incumbent of this historical profile.
