# Neoconservatism: revision 2 research dossier

Research continuation: 4 October 2026. Author: Codex /root/liberal_conservative. Separate review of these additions is pending.

## Scope and evidence boundary

American neoconservatism as synthesised by Irving Kristol (1983–2003), including post-Cold War American primacy and democracy promotion associated with PNAC. Domestic positions use Kristol’s explicit account rather than extrapolation from foreign policy alone. The school differs from libertarian minimal government and nationalist non-interventionism. Neither every member of an administration nor every later conservative is represented.

Kristol’s 1983 interview and 2003 synthesis establish domestic positions independently of PNAC foreign-policy positions: public morality, market coordination and a safety net rather than equalisation. Scope now explicitly includes this intellectual development, rather than attaching all earlier neocon ideas to a solely 1997–2008 foreign-policy statement. Kristol accepts state activity and evolving morality, so maximal libertarian or frozen-tradition answers would be inaccurate. A single coherent empirical anthropology cannot be imported from Wilson without explaining differences among these writers.

The draft contains all 240 current question IDs: 46 supported numeric assessments and 194 unresolved entries. Unknown is not a score. No target placement or neighbouring profile was consulted.
