## Scope and evidence boundary

The political-economic doctrine developed by the Thatcher/Joseph movement, with privatisation, market competition, legal order and national sovereignty. Historical government conduct is contextual evidence rather than an automatic doctrinal position. Revision2 is an incomplete evidence audit of this specified school. Unsupported modern applications and empirical causal comparisons are null/unknown. Neither agreement nor neutrality is inferred from silence. Historical or programme sources are not a complete contemporary incumbent-government record.

The 1976 programme adds specific immigration and welfare instruments; the 1989 UN speech adds binding environmental cooperation and preventive action. These prevent a caricature of unconditional nationalism or technological permissiveness. Some 1976 proposals changed in later government: they establish formation of the doctrine, not permanent commitment to every proposed institution. No current incumbent is represented as implementing historical Thatcherism completely.

The draft contains all 240 current question IDs: 94 supported numeric assessments and 146 unresolved entries. Unknown is not a score. No target placement or neighbouring profile was consulted.
