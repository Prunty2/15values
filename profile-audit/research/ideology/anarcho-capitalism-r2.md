# Anarcho-capitalism (Rothbardian): revision 2 research dossier

Research resumed 2026-10-04. Incomplete research draft; no archive or percentage publication authorised by this dossier.

Scope: The natural-rights market anarchism articulated in For a New Liberty and The Ethics of Liberty, distinguished from Hoppean covenant communities and consequentialist market anarchism. No incumbent government is claimed to implement this doctrine completely. Strict revision-2 re-audit: assess only source-supported positions on the exact questions. Unsupported empirical comparisons, modern technology analogies and institutional particulars remain unknown. Absence of doctrine is not a neutral position. This draft must not be used to calculate or publish a complete profile. Rothbard’s stateless ideal is not silently recast as a transitional parliamentary democracy. Where a question requires a choice between state arrangements he rejects, unresolved institutional fit is disclosed rather than interpreted as endorsement of the opposite pole.

Current coverage: 134 numerical answers and 106 unresolved answers. All 240 exact question records remain in the draft.

## Evidence checked and limitations

For a New Liberty and the primary Education: Free and Compulsory were investigated for acquired values, natural abilities, individual development and policy transition. Education describes learning and individual differences, but does not settle most of the bank’s comparative empirical claims. Welfare incentives are not evidence about inherited racial self-control.

Searches located contemporary Libertarian Forum discussions of tax referenda and political action, but bylines and exact prescriptions were not yet verified sufficiently to transfer those statements to Rothbard. The 1972 LP platform must not be treated automatically as Rothbard’s personal endorsement of every clause.
