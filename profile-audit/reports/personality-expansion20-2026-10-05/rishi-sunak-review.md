## Corrections and limitations

- public-private, question 5: The scoped NHS or New Zealand public hospital system relied substantially on publicly owned healthcare facilities. Public funding is distinct from private ownership, and contracting some services does not imply that facilities should generally be privately owned.

- authority-liberty, question 16: UK detention powers retained judicial constraints and defined legal procedures. The question removes independent court review until officials themselves decide the threat has ended, a stronger power than the scoped system supports.
