## Corrections and limitations

- democracy-autocracy, question 8: A national government chosen by union leaders reproduces an executive ruling structure. Voluntary worker associations are not authorisation for trade union officials to replace the general electorate in choosing a state government.
- nationalism-internationalism, question 2: An international institution enforcing decisions against the freely associated community would reproduce coercive state domination. His international federation was voluntary; internationalist association does not establish unconditional binding bureaucratic authority.
- nationalism-internationalism, question 6: A coercive international court with overriding state-like power conflicts with free federation and rejection of imposed authority. Voluntary arbitration would be different from the question’s binding institutional jurisdiction.
- high-redistribution-low-redistribution, question 2: He opposed coercive state taxation, but also the claim that reducing income differences has no justification. His collective-property programme makes an outright anti-equality rationale less plausible; the rejection is qualified because the question specifically names taxation.
- high-redistribution-low-redistribution, question 6: Retaining individual earnings in a capitalist distributive order despite substantial inequality conflicts with collective economic emancipation. Opposition to state tax does not establish acceptance of entrenched income differences.
- high-redistribution-low-redistribution, question 9: He endorsed material equalisation and opposed concentrated wealth while rejecting a coercive state tax-and-transfer apparatus. The proposition combines an endorsed aim with a contested means; Neutral is a substantive mixed response rather than a substitute for missing evidence.
- culture-nature, question 14: The 1871 letter includes discriminatory collective-character stereotypes, while other writings emphasise socially produced domination and emancipation. Those stereotypes are serious counter-evidence but do not directly document a biological explanation of per-capita violent acts; the competing indications make a mixed response most plausible and especially uncertain.

For every subject, public commitments, coalition implementation and modern analogies were distinguished. Sources were checked against at least three publisher organisations, although Wikipedia synthesises some of the same underlying material and is not independent confirmation of every claim. Some accessible search excerpts and official biographical pages are only contextual, and a third publisher is not independent verification of all answers. Every Culture vs Nature comparison and modern enhancement, AI or admissions mechanism should receive particular human scrutiny. A social reform record is contextual evidence, not a biological doctrine.
