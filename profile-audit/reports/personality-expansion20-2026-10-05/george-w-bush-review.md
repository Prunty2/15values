## Corrections and limitations

- central-local, question 3: Preservation of the federal union is more consistent with requiring broader constitutional authority than with an unrestricted right to leave on a regional majority vote. Stronger national programmes do not determine this answer by themselves.
- central-local, question 5: Constitutional states retained their own governmental constitutions. The question expressly preserves national constitutional rights, so federal programme supremacy does not contradict this protected state role.
- central-local, question 6: Ordinary US policing remained divided among state and local authorities despite federal law-enforcement agencies. A single national directing authority is a broader centralisation than the scoped record supports.
- public-private, question 9: Ordinary commercial ownership was to move towards private enterprise; emergency rescues and public service assets qualify the preference but do not make routine privatisation a mixed aim.
- planning-free-market, question 12: He signed the 2007 federal minimum-wage increase while favouring market allocation in ordinary production. Wage-floor legislation is distinct from economy-wide government wage schedules.
