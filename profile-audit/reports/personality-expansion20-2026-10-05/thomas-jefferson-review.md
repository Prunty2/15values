## Corrections and limitations

- democracy-autocracy, question 3: The word every includes women and other adults excluded by the scoped electoral order. Republican elections alone cannot establish universal adult suffrage, and later positions outside the cutoff are excluded.
- central-local, question 3: Preservation of the federal union is more consistent with requiring broader constitutional authority than with an unrestricted right to leave on a regional majority vote. Stronger national programmes do not determine this answer by themselves.
- central-local, question 5: Constitutional states retained their own governmental constitutions. The question expressly preserves national constitutional rights, so federal programme supremacy does not contradict this protected state role.
- central-local, question 6: Ordinary US policing remained divided among state and local authorities despite federal law-enforcement agencies. A single national directing authority is a broader centralisation than the scoped record supports.
- culture-nature, question 14: His prejudiced racial conjectures concerned capacities and character while admitting limits of knowledge and effects of slavery. They do not directly settle the specific comparative explanation of violent acts. A mixed response recognises the contradictory indications without inventing an exact biological-violence claim.

For every subject, public commitments, coalition implementation and modern analogies were distinguished. Sources were checked against at least three publisher organisations, although Wikipedia synthesises some of the same underlying material and is not independent confirmation of every claim. Some accessible search excerpts and official biographical pages are only contextual, and a third publisher is not independent verification of all answers.
