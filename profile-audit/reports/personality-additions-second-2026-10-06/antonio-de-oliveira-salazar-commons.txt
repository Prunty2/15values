Notes


Official portrait of Salazar. Some pages give different dates of creation for this photograph, in Getty Images there are two copies:


One of them credits Paul Popper as collector and gives April 1968 as date.

The second copy, kept by Keystone, establishes it's inception date between circa 1965 and September 1968.This photograph was held in Portuguese institutions, like schools, during the Estado Novo (see File:Elementos da escola salazarista.JPG) and was used by Coimbra editora in its book António de Oliveira Salazar, Discursos e Notas Políticas.
