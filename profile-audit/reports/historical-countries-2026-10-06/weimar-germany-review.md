## Axis-specific counter-evidence

### democracy-autocracy

Universal adult suffrage, multiparty elections, confidence procedures and national referendum provisions established democracy; emergency presidential rule undermined accountability after 1930.

Assess access to effective governing power, including excluded populations; plebiscites and nominal constitutional rights are not equivalent to competitive control. Representative government does not establish support for binding direct votes.  emergency presidential rule undermined accountability after 1930.

### authority-liberty

Constitutional expression and assembly rights coexisted with emergency decrees, policing and restrictive practice during crises.

Keep ordinary reviewed health, security and policing restrictions distinct from arbitrary political repression. Unequal protection across classes or colonies qualifies generalisation. Online communications are an analogy to period communications. Constitutional expression and assembly rights coexisted with emergency decrees, policing and restrictive practice during crises.

### assimilation-multiculturalism

Equal citizenship and minority protections coexisted with a predominant German national identity and nationalist pressures rather than complete cultural equality.

Distinguish coercive exclusion from assimilation and administrative recognition from equal membership. Apply migrant wording by analogy where the evidence concerns established minorities, with reduced confidence. Equal citizenship and minority protections coexisted with a predominant German national identity and nationalist pressures rather than complete cultural equality.

### restricted-immigration-open-immigration

Admission and labour migration remained regulated, with protection for selected refugees and families rather than unconditional settlement or no deportation.

Separate immigration from emigration, colonial occupation and coerced labour. Modern numerical quotas, welfare entitlements and graduate routes require analogy to actual admission practices. Admission and labour migration remained regulated, with protection for selected refugees and families rather than unconditional settlement or no deportation.

### militarist-pacifist

Treaty limits and diplomacy constrained armed power, while clandestine rearmament and military influence show the republic was not committed to unconditional pacifism.

Separate defensive capacity, actual military deployment and diplomatic restraint. Autonomous AI weapons did not exist; that answer is an uncertain analogy to willingness to delegate military technology and use force. Treaty limits and diplomacy constrained armed power, while clandestine rearmament and military influence show the republic was not committed to unconditional pacifism.

### nationalism-internationalism

League membership, Locarno and treaty cooperation accepted some international constraints while asserting national interests and revising Versailles diplomatically.

Treat cooperation as binding shared authority only where institutions actually constrained the state. Contemporary climate, human-rights and monetary bodies require institutional analogy. League membership, Locarno and treaty cooperation accepted some international constraints while asserting national interests and revising Versailles diplomatically.

### public-private

Private firms remained important alongside public rail, utilities, broadcasting and municipal services; constitutional socialisation powers did not nationalise the whole economy.

Measure legal or social ownership, not whether private firms were regulated. Modern service-contracting and broadcasting questions require comparison with period public services; social ownership is not ordinary private capital.  constitutional socialisation powers did not nationalise the whole economy.

### protectionism-free-trade

Trade remained substantial but tariffs and depression-era controls protected domestic interests; free-trade aspirations were qualified by economic crisis.

Do not confuse export activity with open imports or forced sanctions with a voluntary policy. Online-service trade is analogous to cross-border commercial access, not a historical digital policy.  free-trade aspirations were qualified by economic crisis.

### planning-free-market

Market production coexisted with wage bargaining, public regulation, rent controls and economic intervention; crisis policies differed from permanent comprehensive output planning.

Measure production, price, investment and wage direction separately from ownership. Private currency and central-bank mechanisms require period institutional comparison; absence of a modern institution is not an automatic answer.  crisis policies differed from permanent comprehensive output planning.

### high-redistribution-low-redistribution

Social insurance, unemployment protection and progressive taxation expanded redistributive provision, but benefits were reduced under depression-era austerity.

Public services and spending do not establish equalising cash transfers. Compare eligible groups and actual taxation, relief, inheritance and land policy; compensation and modern welfare language may require approximation. Social insurance, unemployment protection and progressive taxation expanded redistributive provision, but benefits were reduced under depression-era austerity.

### secular-religious

The constitution rejected a state church while retaining religious instruction, recognised church corporations and public holidays; formal disestablishment was not removal of every religious privilege.

Measure religion in state law and institutions rather than population belief or a leader’s private faith. Civilian policy power, religious schooling, holidays, marriage and clerical offices can point in different directions.  formal disestablishment was not removal of every religious privilege.

### progressive-traditionalist

Social liberalisation and women's formal civic equality coexisted with conservative marriage norms and criminal restrictions on homosexuality and abortion.

Distinguish gender equality and selected reforms from present-day recognition of same-sex marriage or gender identity. Apply family and sexual norms separately; parents’ control may conflict with state-directed schooling. Social liberalisation and women's formal civic equality coexisted with conservative marriage norms and criminal restrictions on homosexuality and abortion.

### innovation-caution

Modern science, industrial engineering, housing and infrastructure were promoted alongside regulatory safeguards; advanced enhancement technologies are analogies.

Separate material development from an unrestricted willingness to accept irreversible harms. AI, cultivated meat, implants, climate technology and embryo editing are explicitly historical analogies, not documented period proposals.  advanced enhancement technologies are analogies.

### central-local

Lander retained constitutions and legislative powers while Reich law, fiscal authority and emergency intervention limited autonomy.

Distinguish formal constitutional autonomy, local administration and effective party or imperial control. Customary institutions are not necessarily protected self-government; contemporary treatment and tax mechanisms require analogy. Lander retained constitutions and legislative powers while Reich law, fiscal authority and emergency intervention limited autonomy.

### culture-nature

Education and social reform coexisted with contemporary eugenics and inherited-trait theories; no uniform democratic-state position on every modern psychological claim can be assumed.

This axis concerns causes of traits, not environmental policy or whether groups received equal rights. Education and moral cultivation provide limited evidence for learned formation; hierarchy does not prove a genetic claim about every trait. Specific modern IQ, stress, empathy and attention hypotheses remain low-confidence analogies.  no uniform democratic-state position on every modern psychological claim can be assumed.
