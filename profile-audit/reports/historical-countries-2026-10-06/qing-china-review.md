## Axis-specific counter-evidence

### democracy-autocracy

The emperor and court governed through appointed officials; late provincial assemblies and proposed constitutional reform did not establish regular competitive national executive elections.

Assess access to effective governing power, including excluded populations; plebiscites and nominal constitutional rights are not equivalent to competitive control. Representative government does not establish support for binding direct votes.  late provincial assemblies and proposed constitutional reform did not establish regular competitive national executive elections.

### authority-liberty

Imperial policing, censorship and status-based legal restrictions limited liberty, while reform codes and local practices created uneven protections.

Keep ordinary reviewed health, security and policing restrictions distinct from arbitrary political repression. Unequal protection across classes or colonies qualifies generalisation. Online communications are an analogy to period communications. Imperial policing, censorship and status-based legal restrictions limited liberty, while reform codes and local practices created uneven protections.

### assimilation-multiculturalism

Manchu-Han distinctions and Confucian cultural expectations coexisted with multiethnic imperial institutions and separate frontier administration.

Distinguish coercive exclusion from assimilation and administrative recognition from equal membership. Apply migrant wording by analogy where the evidence concerns established minorities, with reduced confidence. Manchu-Han distinctions and Confucian cultural expectations coexisted with multiethnic imperial institutions and separate frontier administration.

### restricted-immigration-open-immigration

Treaty-port foreigners and controlled settlement operated under unequal treaty arrangements; forced concessions did not establish a general government preference for open immigration.

Separate immigration from emigration, colonial occupation and coerced labour. Modern numerical quotas, welfare entitlements and graduate routes require analogy to actual admission practices.  forced concessions did not establish a general government preference for open immigration.

### militarist-pacifist

Military modernisation and territorial defence were central, but defeats and treaty concessions also produced pragmatic diplomatic restraint.

Separate defensive capacity, actual military deployment and diplomatic restraint. Autonomous AI weapons did not exist; that answer is an uncertain analogy to willingness to delegate military technology and use force. Military modernisation and territorial defence were central, but defeats and treaty concessions also produced pragmatic diplomatic restraint.

### nationalism-internationalism

The dynasty resisted loss of sovereignty under unequal treaties, despite accepting foreign concessions and diplomatic institutions.

Treat cooperation as binding shared authority only where institutions actually constrained the state. Contemporary climate, human-rights and monetary bodies require institutional analogy. The dynasty resisted loss of sovereignty under unequal treaties, despite accepting foreign concessions and diplomatic institutions.

### public-private

Private farming and commerce predominated alongside arsenals and government-supervised enterprises; late industrial investment mixed public and merchant finance.

Measure legal or social ownership, not whether private firms were regulated. Modern service-contracting and broadcasting questions require comparison with period public services; social ownership is not ordinary private capital.  late industrial investment mixed public and merchant finance.

### protectionism-free-trade

Treaty tariffs and foreign access constrained protection, while officials sought domestic productive strength; imposed market access differs from principled unilateral free trade.

Do not confuse export activity with open imports or forced sanctions with a voluntary policy. Online-service trade is analogous to cross-border commercial access, not a historical digital policy.  imposed market access differs from principled unilateral free trade.

### planning-free-market

Self-strengthening programmes directed strategic investment while private agricultural and commercial markets continued without general compulsory production quotas.

Measure production, price, investment and wage direction separately from ownership. Private currency and central-bank mechanisms require period institutional comparison; absence of a modern institution is not an automatic answer. Self-strengthening programmes directed strategic investment while private agricultural and commercial markets continued without general compulsory production quotas.

### high-redistribution-low-redistribution

Relief, granaries and land-tax administration addressed subsistence and order without broadly equalising household incomes or abolishing large private holdings.

Public services and spending do not establish equalising cash transfers. Compare eligible groups and actual taxation, relief, inheritance and land policy; compensation and modern welfare language may require approximation. Relief, granaries and land-tax administration addressed subsistence and order without broadly equalising household incomes or abolishing large private holdings.

### secular-religious

Confucian state ritual and imperial-sacred legitimacy coexisted with civil administration, several religions and no single clerical legislative veto.

Measure religion in state law and institutions rather than population belief or a leader’s private faith. Civilian policy power, religious schooling, holidays, marriage and clerical offices can point in different directions. Confucian state ritual and imperial-sacred legitimacy coexisted with civil administration, several religions and no single clerical legislative veto.

### progressive-traditionalist

Family hierarchy, patriarchal marriage and established moral conventions persisted; late reform of education and administration did not imply contemporary sexual equality.

Distinguish gender equality and selected reforms from present-day recognition of same-sex marriage or gender identity. Apply family and sexual norms separately; parents’ control may conflict with state-directed schooling.  late reform of education and administration did not imply contemporary sexual equality.

### innovation-caution

Self-strengthening adopted weapons, mines and railways selectively to preserve established institutions; uptake and funding varied strongly by province.

Separate material development from an unrestricted willingness to accept irreversible harms. AI, cultivated meat, implants, climate technology and embryo editing are explicitly historical analogies, not documented period proposals.  uptake and funding varied strongly by province.

### central-local

Central imperial authority coexisted with strengthened provincial armies and finances after rebellions; practical decentralisation did not establish a protected regional right to secede.

Distinguish formal constitutional autonomy, local administration and effective party or imperial control. Customary institutions are not necessarily protected self-government; contemporary treatment and tax mechanisms require analogy.  practical decentralisation did not establish a protected regional right to secede.

### culture-nature

Confucian learning and moral cultivation provide evidence for social formation; lineage and ethnic distinctions qualify this, without proving modern racial genetic claims.

This axis concerns causes of traits, not environmental policy or whether groups received equal rights. Education and moral cultivation provide limited evidence for learned formation; hierarchy does not prove a genetic claim about every trait. Specific modern IQ, stress, empathy and attention hypotheses remain low-confidence analogies.  lineage and ethnic distinctions qualify this, without proving modern racial genetic claims.
