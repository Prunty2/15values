## Axis-specific counter-evidence

### democracy-autocracy

The hereditary emperor, controlled legislative bodies and managed plebiscites concentrated authority; ratification votes were not regular competitive leadership elections.

Assess access to effective governing power, including excluded populations; plebiscites and nominal constitutional rights are not equivalent to competitive control. Representative government does not establish support for binding direct votes.  ratification votes were not regular competitive leadership elections.

### authority-liberty

Police supervision, censorship and restrictions on association subordinated political liberty to imperial stability, despite codified civil property protections.

Keep ordinary reviewed health, security and policing restrictions distinct from arbitrary political repression. Unequal protection across classes or colonies qualifies generalisation. Online communications are an analogy to period communications. Police supervision, censorship and restrictions on association subordinated political liberty to imperial stability, despite codified civil property protections.

### assimilation-multiculturalism

Uniform French civil institutions and language promoted integration, while recognised religious communities survived under central supervision.

Distinguish coercive exclusion from assimilation and administrative recognition from equal membership. Apply migrant wording by analogy where the evidence concerns established minorities, with reduced confidence. Uniform French civil institutions and language promoted integration, while recognised religious communities survived under central supervision.

### restricted-immigration-open-immigration

Foreign entry and residence were administratively controlled in a wartime empire; movement through annexed lands was not an equal international migration right.

Separate immigration from emigration, colonial occupation and coerced labour. Modern numerical quotas, welfare entitlements and graduate routes require analogy to actual admission practices.  movement through annexed lands was not an equal international migration right.

### militarist-pacifist

Conscription, military expansion and repeated campaigns made force central, alongside treaties used to consolidate victories.

Separate defensive capacity, actual military deployment and diplomatic restraint. Autonomous AI weapons did not exist; that answer is an uncertain analogy to willingness to delegate military technology and use force. Conscription, military expansion and repeated campaigns made force central, alongside treaties used to consolidate victories.

### nationalism-internationalism

Imperial sovereignty and subordinate allied kingdoms dominated diplomacy; an imposed continental empire differs from shared authority among equal states.

Treat cooperation as binding shared authority only where institutions actually constrained the state. Contemporary climate, human-rights and monetary bodies require institutional analogy.  an imposed continental empire differs from shared authority among equal states.

### public-private

Private property and businesses were protected under the Civil Code, with public infrastructure and strategic institutions alongside them.

Measure legal or social ownership, not whether private firms were regulated. Modern service-contracting and broadcasting questions require comparison with period public services; social ownership is not ordinary private capital. Private property and businesses were protected under the Civil Code, with public infrastructure and strategic institutions alongside them.

### protectionism-free-trade

The Continental System restricted British commerce and directed European trade for imperial strategy; smuggling and selective trade persisted.

Do not confuse export activity with open imports or forced sanctions with a voluntary policy. Online-service trade is analogous to cross-border commercial access, not a historical digital policy.  smuggling and selective trade persisted.

### planning-free-market

Private production continued with administrative regulation, strategic subsidies, central-bank support and wartime direction rather than a socialist production plan.

Measure production, price, investment and wage direction separately from ownership. Private currency and central-bank mechanisms require period institutional comparison; absence of a modern institution is not an automatic answer. Private production continued with administrative regulation, strategic subsidies, central-bank support and wartime direction rather than a socialist production plan.

### high-redistribution-low-redistribution

Property rights and tax administration preserved unequal holdings; revolutionary changes to tenure and limited relief did not create extensive income transfers.

Public services and spending do not establish equalising cash transfers. Compare eligible groups and actual taxation, relief, inheritance and land policy; compensation and modern welfare language may require approximation.  revolutionary changes to tenure and limited relief did not create extensive income transfers.

### secular-religious

The Concordat recognised Catholic worship and public clergy support while preserving civil law and subjecting churches to state supervision.

Measure religion in state law and institutions rather than population belief or a leader’s private faith. Civilian policy power, religious schooling, holidays, marriage and clerical offices can point in different directions. The Concordat recognised Catholic worship and public clergy support while preserving civil law and subjecting churches to state supervision.

### progressive-traditionalist

The Civil Code restored strong paternal and marital authority, while codified civil status and some revolutionary legal changes departed from older arrangements.

Distinguish gender equality and selected reforms from present-day recognition of same-sex marriage or gender identity. Apply family and sexual norms separately; parents’ control may conflict with state-directed schooling. The Civil Code restored strong paternal and marital authority, while codified civil status and some revolutionary legal changes departed from older arrangements.

### innovation-caution

Scientific institutions, technical education and infrastructure supported development, while many modern environmental and biomedical questions lack exact counterparts.

Separate material development from an unrestricted willingness to accept irreversible harms. AI, cultivated meat, implants, climate technology and embryo editing are explicitly historical analogies, not documented period proposals. Scientific institutions, technical education and infrastructure supported development, while many modern environmental and biomedical questions lack exact counterparts.

### central-local

Prefects, centrally organised law and administration subordinated regional government, although imperial territories differed in legal arrangements.

Distinguish formal constitutional autonomy, local administration and effective party or imperial control. Customary institutions are not necessarily protected self-government; contemporary treatment and tax mechanisms require analogy. Prefects, centrally organised law and administration subordinated regional government, although imperial territories differed in legal arrangements.

### culture-nature

Legal education and civic formation suggest social influences, but status, gender and colonial racial hierarchy persisted; precise psychological heredity claims remain uncertain.

This axis concerns causes of traits, not environmental policy or whether groups received equal rights. Education and moral cultivation provide limited evidence for learned formation; hierarchy does not prove a genetic claim about every trait. Specific modern IQ, stress, empathy and attention hypotheses remain low-confidence analogies.  precise psychological heredity claims remain uncertain.
