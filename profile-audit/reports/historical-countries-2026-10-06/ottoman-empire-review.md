## Axis-specific counter-evidence

### democracy-autocracy

Tanzimat reforms were issued by the sultan and appointed ministers; the 1876 constitution ended the chosen period and does not establish sustained parliamentary accountability during it.

Assess access to effective governing power, including excluded populations; plebiscites and nominal constitutional rights are not equivalent to competitive control. Representative government does not establish support for binding direct votes.  the 1876 constitution ended the chosen period and does not establish sustained parliamentary accountability during it.

### authority-liberty

New guarantees of life, property and trial coexisted with censorship and central police power; reform curtailed some arbitrary practice without establishing broad autonomous expression.

Keep ordinary reviewed health, security and policing restrictions distinct from arbitrary political repression. Unequal protection across classes or colonies qualifies generalisation. Online communications are an analogy to period communications.  reform curtailed some arbitrary practice without establishing broad autonomous expression.

### assimilation-multiculturalism

Ottomanism sought a shared imperial citizenship while recognising religious communities and languages; communal equality remained uneven in implementation.

Distinguish coercive exclusion from assimilation and administrative recognition from equal membership. Apply migrant wording by analogy where the evidence concerns established minorities, with reduced confidence.  communal equality remained uneven in implementation.

### restricted-immigration-open-immigration

The empire admitted large numbers of Muslim refugees and encouraged settlement, while status and security remained selective; humanitarian reception was not universally race- and faith-neutral migration.

Separate immigration from emigration, colonial occupation and coerced labour. Modern numerical quotas, welfare entitlements and graduate routes require analogy to actual admission practices.  humanitarian reception was not universally race- and faith-neutral migration.

### militarist-pacifist

Army reform, conscription and the Crimean War strengthened military capability, alongside reliance on European diplomatic guarantees.

Separate defensive capacity, actual military deployment and diplomatic restraint. Autonomous AI weapons did not exist; that answer is an uncertain analogy to willingness to delegate military technology and use force. Army reform, conscription and the Crimean War strengthened military capability, alongside reliance on European diplomatic guarantees.

### nationalism-internationalism

The empire defended sovereignty through treaty diplomacy and the Concert of Europe, accepting concessions without yielding general final authority to supranational bodies.

Treat cooperation as binding shared authority only where institutions actually constrained the state. Contemporary climate, human-rights and monetary bodies require institutional analogy. The empire defended sovereignty through treaty diplomacy and the Concert of Europe, accepting concessions without yielding general final authority to supranational bodies.

### public-private

Private property and trade coexisted with state land, arsenals, public enterprises and partly foreign-owned infrastructure; mixed ownership differed from socialist nationalisation.

Measure legal or social ownership, not whether private firms were regulated. Modern service-contracting and broadcasting questions require comparison with period public services; social ownership is not ordinary private capital.  mixed ownership differed from socialist nationalisation.

### protectionism-free-trade

Commercial treaties lowered barriers and admitted foreign merchants, often under outside pressure; efforts to develop local industry persisted.

Do not confuse export activity with open imports or forced sanctions with a voluntary policy. Online-service trade is analogous to cross-border commercial access, not a historical digital policy.  efforts to develop local industry persisted.

### planning-free-market

Reforms promoted commercial codes and private enterprise alongside centrally sponsored industrialisation and administrative direction.

Measure production, price, investment and wage direction separately from ownership. Private currency and central-bank mechanisms require period institutional comparison; absence of a modern institution is not an automatic answer. Reforms promoted commercial codes and private enterprise alongside centrally sponsored industrialisation and administrative direction.

### high-redistribution-low-redistribution

Tax and administrative reforms aimed at state finance and order rather than extensive income equalisation; religious charity and limited relief remained important.

Public services and spending do not establish equalising cash transfers. Compare eligible groups and actual taxation, relief, inheritance and land policy; compensation and modern welfare language may require approximation.  religious charity and limited relief remained important.

### secular-religious

Islam and the sultan-caliph's religious legitimacy remained foundations, with sharia and millet family jurisdiction alongside new civil-commercial courts.

Measure religion in state law and institutions rather than population belief or a leader’s private faith. Civilian policy power, religious schooling, holidays, marriage and clerical offices can point in different directions. Islam and the sultan-caliph's religious legitimacy remained foundations, with sharia and millet family jurisdiction alongside new civil-commercial courts.

### progressive-traditionalist

Imperial and religious family norms remained patriarchal; administrative modernisation did not make same-sex marriage or contemporary gender recognition adopted policies.

Distinguish gender equality and selected reforms from present-day recognition of same-sex marriage or gender identity. Apply family and sexual norms separately; parents’ control may conflict with state-directed schooling.  administrative modernisation did not make same-sex marriage or contemporary gender recognition adopted policies.

### innovation-caution

Telegraphy, railways, new schools, medicine and military factories encouraged technological catch-up, constrained by finance and uneven implementation.

Separate material development from an unrestricted willingness to accept irreversible harms. AI, cultivated meat, implants, climate technology and embryo editing are explicitly historical analogies, not documented period proposals. Telegraphy, railways, new schools, medicine and military factories encouraged technological catch-up, constrained by finance and uneven implementation.

### central-local

Provincial reform and new ministries strengthened central administration while recognised religious communities and local notables retained influence.

Distinguish formal constitutional autonomy, local administration and effective party or imperial control. Customary institutions are not necessarily protected self-government; contemporary treatment and tax mechanisms require analogy. Provincial reform and new ministries strengthened central administration while recognised religious communities and local notables retained influence.

### culture-nature

Reform schooling and shared citizenship stressed social formation; descent and confessional distinctions remained, but no exact stance on modern behavioural genetics follows.

This axis concerns causes of traits, not environmental policy or whether groups received equal rights. Education and moral cultivation provide limited evidence for learned formation; hierarchy does not prove a genetic claim about every trait. Specific modern IQ, stress, empathy and attention hypotheses remain low-confidence analogies.  descent and confessional distinctions remained, but no exact stance on modern behavioural genetics follows.
