## Axis-specific counter-evidence

### democracy-autocracy

The Rhodesian Front governed through Westminster institutions, but property, income and educational qualifications and racial seat allocation excluded the Black majority from equal electoral power.

Assess access to effective governing power, including excluded populations; plebiscites and nominal constitutional rights are not equivalent to competitive control. Representative government does not establish support for binding direct votes. The Rhodesian Front governed through Westminster institutions, but property, income and educational qualifications and racial seat allocation excluded the Black majority from equal electoral power.

### authority-liberty

Emergency regulations, detention, censorship and racial movement controls coexisted with ordinary legal protections and substantial freedoms for white residents.

Keep ordinary reviewed health, security and policing restrictions distinct from arbitrary political repression. Unequal protection across classes or colonies qualifies generalisation. Online communications are an analogy to period communications. Emergency regulations, detention, censorship and racial movement controls coexisted with ordinary legal protections and substantial freedoms for white residents.

### assimilation-multiculturalism

State institutions privileged European culture and segregated African communities rather than offering equal multicultural membership; customary tribal institutions survived under supervision.

Distinguish coercive exclusion from assimilation and administrative recognition from equal membership. Apply migrant wording by analogy where the evidence concerns established minorities, with reduced confidence.  customary tribal institutions survived under supervision.

### restricted-immigration-open-immigration

The state recruited white settlers and skilled immigrants while excluding an equal, race-neutral settlement entitlement; favourable white immigration was an economic policy rather than general openness.

Separate immigration from emigration, colonial occupation and coerced labour. Modern numerical quotas, welfare entitlements and graduate routes require analogy to actual admission practices.  favourable white immigration was an economic policy rather than general openness.

### militarist-pacifist

Conscription, counterinsurgency and cross-border raids supported the minority government during the Bush War; settlement talks were pursued without abandoning military operations.

Separate defensive capacity, actual military deployment and diplomatic restraint. Autonomous AI weapons did not exist; that answer is an uncertain analogy to willingness to delegate military technology and use force.  settlement talks were pursued without abandoning military operations.

### nationalism-internationalism

UDI and resistance to UN sanctions asserted domestic sovereignty against international demands, while South African support remained important.

Treat cooperation as binding shared authority only where institutions actually constrained the state. Contemporary climate, human-rights and monetary bodies require institutional analogy. UDI and resistance to UN sanctions asserted domestic sovereignty against international demands, while South African support remained important.

### public-private

Commercial farming, mining and manufacturing were mainly private, alongside state railways, utilities, broadcasting and development institutions.

Measure legal or social ownership, not whether private firms were regulated. Modern service-contracting and broadcasting questions require comparison with period public services; social ownership is not ordinary private capital. Commercial farming, mining and manufacturing were mainly private, alongside state railways, utilities, broadcasting and development institutions.

### protectionism-free-trade

Sanctions prompted import substitution and protective controls, though exporters relied on external markets and sanctions evasion; enforced isolation was not a voluntary rejection of all trade.

Do not confuse export activity with open imports or forced sanctions with a voluntary policy. Online-service trade is analogous to cross-border commercial access, not a historical digital policy.  enforced isolation was not a voluntary rejection of all trade.

### planning-free-market

Private firms operated within exchange controls, agricultural regulation and sanctions-era economic direction rather than comprehensive production planning.

Measure production, price, investment and wage direction separately from ownership. Private currency and central-bank mechanisms require period institutional comparison; absence of a modern institution is not an automatic answer. Private firms operated within exchange controls, agricultural regulation and sanctions-era economic direction rather than comprehensive production planning.

### high-redistribution-low-redistribution

Public provision favoured the white minority and protected racially unequal land and income distribution; subsidies and welfare existed without broad equalising transfers.

Public services and spending do not establish equalising cash transfers. Compare eligible groups and actual taxation, relief, inheritance and land policy; compensation and modern welfare language may require approximation.  subsidies and welfare existed without broad equalising transfers.

### secular-religious

Christian institutions and mission schools were influential, but civilian law and parliamentary government did not require clerical approval; customary family law persisted.

Measure religion in state law and institutions rather than population belief or a leader’s private faith. Civilian policy power, religious schooling, holidays, marriage and clerical offices can point in different directions.  customary family law persisted.

### progressive-traditionalist

The government sustained patriarchal, socially conservative institutions and racially separated family and educational norms.

Distinguish gender equality and selected reforms from present-day recognition of same-sex marriage or gender identity. Apply family and sexual norms separately; parents’ control may conflict with state-directed schooling. The government sustained patriarchal, socially conservative institutions and racially separated family and educational norms.

### innovation-caution

Mining, industrialisation, infrastructure and commercial agriculture favoured material development; exact contemporary AI, implant and gene-editing positions are historical analogies.

Separate material development from an unrestricted willingness to accept irreversible harms. AI, cultivated meat, implants, climate technology and embryo editing are explicitly historical analogies, not documented period proposals.  exact contemporary AI, implant and gene-editing positions are historical analogies.

### central-local

Salisbury controlled national security and policy while rural African affairs used subordinated chiefs and local institutions, not sovereign constituent states.

Distinguish formal constitutional autonomy, local administration and effective party or imperial control. Customary institutions are not necessarily protected self-government; contemporary treatment and tax mechanisms require analogy. Salisbury controlled national security and policy while rural African affairs used subordinated chiefs and local institutions, not sovereign constituent states.

### culture-nature

Race-based institutional hierarchy suggests essentialist assumptions, while schools and assimilation programmes acknowledged social formation; this does not document a biological opinion about every psychological trait.

This axis concerns causes of traits, not environmental policy or whether groups received equal rights. Education and moral cultivation provide limited evidence for learned formation; hierarchy does not prove a genetic claim about every trait. Specific modern IQ, stress, empathy and attention hypotheses remain low-confidence analogies.  this does not document a biological opinion about every psychological trait.
