## Corrections and limitations

- Axis 13, question 7: Vegetarian concern about animal harm could favour replacing slaughtered meat, while criticism of industrial machinery weighs against an early large-scale technical substitute. The combined benefit-and-risk claim plausibly elicits a mixed response.
- Axis 15, question 3: His writings assigned distinctive maternal and social capacities to women as well as promoting education and public action. This is weak evidence for an essentialist account of some sex differences and qualifies an automatic social-only explanation.

Nehru’s party-ban interpretation was additionally corrected after reading his 16 April 1949 letter: central Cabinet avoidance of a ban distinguishes dissenting views from alleged violent acts. For every subject, public commitments, coalition implementation and modern analogies were distinguished. Sources were checked against at least three publisher organisations, although Wikipedia synthesises some of the same underlying material and is not independent confirmation of every claim. Every Culture vs Nature comparison and modern enhancement, AI or admissions mechanism should receive particular human scrutiny. A social reform record is contextual evidence, not a biological doctrine.
