## Corrections and limitations

- Axis 8, question 5: The defence-related navigation exception supplies a real counterweight to cheaper imports. Essential goods include both ordinary necessities and strategic defence supplies, making a conditional response to this broad formulation plausible.
- Axis 8, question 11: He accepted retaliatory duties where they were likely to produce removal of a foreign barrier but rejected costly retaliation without that prospect. The question omits the condition, so a mixed response is preferable.
- Axis 10, question 5: His discussion of taxes on expensive housing permits richer citizens to contribute more than proportionately. Agreement is qualified because it does not establish every modern substantially progressive income-tax schedule.

Nehru’s party-ban interpretation was additionally corrected after reading his 16 April 1949 letter: central Cabinet avoidance of a ban distinguishes dissenting views from alleged violent acts. For every subject, public commitments, coalition implementation and modern analogies were distinguished. Sources were checked against at least three publisher organisations, although Wikipedia synthesises some of the same underlying material and is not independent confirmation of every claim. Every Culture vs Nature comparison and modern enhancement, AI or admissions mechanism should receive particular human scrutiny. A social reform record is contextual evidence, not a biological doctrine.
